Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

Smart PDF Tools respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Smart PDF Tools mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Smart PDF Tools does not collect personal information such as name, email, or contact details.

The app may access files on your device only when you choose to select PDFs or images for processing (merge, split, compress, convert, etc.).

2. File Access & Storage

Files selected inside the app are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or share your files on external servers.

3. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage permission – to read and save PDF files
  • Media access – to convert images into PDF

4. Ads & Third-Party Services

Smart PDF Tools may display ads using third-party advertising services. These services may collect anonymous usage data to improve ad experience.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. Since files are processed locally, your documents remain under your control.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected on this page.
